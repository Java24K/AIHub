Artify AI is an AI photo transformation app. It allows users to select photos, apply artistic styles, generate images, and save or share results.

Photo Processing

When you submit a photo for generation, the selected photo and the generation instructions are uploaded to our servers and sent to OpenAI to produce the requested image. Uploaded photos and generated images may be stored in our cloud storage to support processing and access to results. Photo generation is not performed entirely on your device.

Information We Collect

We process selected photos, generation instructions, generated images, and generation history. We also store guest identifiers, device fingerprints, credit balances, and purchase records to provide the service and help prevent misuse.

If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier, email address, and available profile information, such as your name and profile photo. We use this information to identify your account and associate it with your credits and purchases. We do not receive your Google password.

Our infrastructure may record technical information, including IP addresses, request timestamps, and error logs, for security and troubleshooting.

How We Use Information

We use information to generate images, manage accounts and credits, verify purchases, provide access to results, resolve support requests, and maintain the security and reliability of the app.

Third-Party Services

Artify AI uses OpenAI for AI image processing, Google Sign-In for authentication, Google Play Billing for purchases, and cloud infrastructure providers, including Google Cloud and Cloudflare R2, for hosting and storage.

These providers process information necessary to deliver their services under their applicable policies and agreements. Google Play handles payment details; we receive purchase information needed to verify transactions and provide purchased benefits.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with service providers as necessary to operate the app. We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect users and prevent fraud.

When you use the app’s sharing feature, the selected image is sent to the destination you choose.

Data Retention and Deletion

Settings and cached files may be stored locally on your device. Account information, uploaded photos, generated images, and transaction records may also be stored on our servers.

Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete server-side information. You may contact zf772835869@88.com to request deletion of your account and associated data. Certain records may need to be retained for legal, transaction, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

Security and International Processing

We use encrypted connections and access controls to help protect information. Service providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children
