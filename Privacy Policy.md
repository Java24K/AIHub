LiteCompress is a photo compression app. The app helps users select photos, compress images locally on the device, save compressed results, and view compression history.

Photo processing:
LiteCompress processes selected photos locally on the user’s device. We do not upload users’ photos to our own servers.

Data collection:
LiteCompress itself does not require users to create an account and does not collect personal account information. The app may store compression history and settings locally on the device to provide app features.

Third-party services:
The app uses Google AdMob to show ads and Google Play Billing to process purchases. These Google services may collect device identifiers, advertising data, purchase information, diagnostics, and usage data according to Google’s policies.

Data sharing:
We do not sell users’ personal information. Data processed by Google AdMob and Google Play Billing is handled by Google according to their own privacy policies.

Data retention:
Local app data, such as settings and compression history, remains on the user’s device unless the user deletes it or uninstalls the app.

Children:
LiteCompress is not specifically directed to children.
